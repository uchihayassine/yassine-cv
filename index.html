My Story

I learned to code young before AI became a daily utility. Then I watched it arrive, in a window I can actually point to. It changed my workflows, my instincts, and eventually my ambitions. Experiencing that paradigm shift firsthand changed how I approach engineering and, ultimately, what I want to achieve.

The future is built by those who spot the next technological shift before it becomes industry standard. My goal isn't just to leverage what gets built, but to architect the foundations of it. My current focus is on Agentic AI, data engineering, and scalable cloud infrastructure. I'm most interested in the friction points: the exact intersection where theoretical reasoning systems meet real-world production constraints.

If you're working on the infrastructure, models, or applications of the next era, I'd love to hear about it.
